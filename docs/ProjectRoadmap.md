# 📦 InventoryPro - Project Roadmap

## Vision

InventoryPro is a modern inventory management platform designed for small and medium businesses.

Our goal is to simplify inventory management, billing, analytics, employee management, and AI-powered business insights through one powerful application.

---

# Project Objectives

- Easy inventory management
- Fast billing
- Barcode & QR support
- Multi-store management
- Employee management
- Customer management
- Supplier management
- AI-powered analytics
- Cloud synchronization
- Offline support
- Secure authentication
- Beautiful modern UI
